# 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information.

## A. Account and Profile Information

We may collect information that you provide when creating or maintaining a Ready2Golf account, including:

* Real name;
* Email address;
* Password credentials in securely hashed form;
* Phone number, if supported;
* Date of birth or age information, if supported;
* Gender, if supported;
* Profile photograph;
* Golf handicap;
* Golf skill level;
* Home golf course, if supported;
* Profile preferences; and
* Other golf-related or account information you choose to provide.

Some categories listed above may not be collected by the current version of Ready2Golf but may be collected if corresponding features are introduced.

We will provide appropriate notice or obtain consent where required before materially changing our collection or use of personal information.

## B. Scheduled Round Information

When a user posts a scheduled golf round, certain information may become visible to other Ready2Golf users, including:

* Name;
* Profile photograph;
* Golf course;
* Date and time of the round;
* Number of available player spots;
* Handicap;
* Skill level; and
* Approximate/current distance from the round, where location permission is enabled.

Ready2Golf rounds may contain up to four golfers.

Information associated with a posted round may remain associated with that round even if the user later changes corresponding information on their general profile.

Users should consider this before posting or joining a round.

## C. Location Information

Ready2Golf may request access to your device's GPS location when the app launches.

Location is used to determine your approximate distance from scheduled golf rounds.

Ready2Golf does **not currently continuously track your GPS location in the background**.

The GPS location used for this distance functionality is used temporarily and is not intended to be stored as a persistent location history in Ready2Golf's database.

You may deny or disable location permission through your device settings. Ready2Golf will remain usable, although features that require location, such as displaying your distance from a scheduled round, may not function.

## D. Group Chat and Messages

Each scheduled round may include a group chat for participating golfers.

We collect and process messages sent through these chats.

Messages currently consist of text. Messages cannot currently be edited or deleted by users after they have been sent.

We may retain messages where reasonably necessary for:

* User safety;
* Investigating reports;
* Preventing harassment or abuse;
* Fraud prevention;
* Enforcement of our Terms;
* Resolving disputes;
* Compliance with legal obligations; or
* Protecting Ready2Golf, our users, or others.

## E. Reports and Blocking Information

If you report or block another user, we may collect information concerning:

* The reporting or blocking user;
* The reported or blocked account;
* The reason for a report;
* Relevant account or round activity;
* Relevant messages;
* Investigation information; and
* Enforcement actions.

Ready2Golf administrators may review relevant information when investigating safety, abuse, fraud, or Terms violations.

## F. Device and Technical Information

Ready2Golf and its service providers may automatically receive certain technical information, including, where applicable:

* Mobile advertising identifiers;
* Push notification tokens or identifiers;
* Device or app identifiers;
* App version;
* Device and operating-system information;
* IP address;
* Time and time-zone information;
* Diagnostic information;
* Crash information;
* Usage information; and
* Other technical information generated through the operation of the Services.

## G. Usage and Analytics Information

We use analytics and diagnostic technologies, including Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.

These services may collect information regarding how the application is used, application sessions, device characteristics, app versions, identifiers, crashes, errors, and other diagnostic or usage information.

## H. Subscription Information

Ready2Golf uses Apple App Store and Google Play billing for premium subscriptions and uses RevenueCat to help manage subscription status and entitlements.

We may receive information such as:

* Whether you have an active subscription;
* Subscription product or plan;
* Subscription status;
* Purchase or renewal status;
* Expiration information;
* Trial status; and
* Transaction or entitlement identifiers.

Ready2Golf does not directly process your complete payment-card information when your subscription is purchased through Apple or Google.
